How to read this page

  • IC (window): Pearson correlation between the channel's window-mean value and the forward return label, over all 545,341 training samples. Positive = bullish-when-high. Magnitudes > 0.03 are notable in equity-prediction land.
  • Per-fold IC: the same correlation computed on each of the three walk-forward validation folds (oldest / middle / newest). A channel whose IC flips sign between folds is non-stationary and unsafe to weight heavily.
  • stab: min(|per-fold IC|) — higher means the channel keeps its sign across regimes.
  • Gain (prod / exp): total XGBoost split gain summed across the channel's lag features. 0.0% means the booster never chose to split on any lag of this channel — pure dead weight in that model, regardless of its raw IC.
  • Coverage: fraction of (sample, lag) cells with a non-zero value. Macro channels show ~55% because they're sparse before backfill caught up; SOURCE channels show ~5% because most rows are blanks (analyst recos don't change every cycle).
